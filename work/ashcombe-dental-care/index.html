Ashcombe Dental Care

A calm, trust-first website for a family dental practice, designed and built in Webflow to make booking a visit feel easy.

Services
Web design & development
Platform
Webflow
Industry
Dental care
Scope

What I
delivered

Dental visits make many people anxious, so every part of this site was designed to feel calm, clear and reassuring, with booking always one step away.

View the site ↗ Email me ↗
  1. 01 A calm visual language: soft blues, a teal accent, editorial serif headings and arched image frames that feel warm rather than clinical.
  2. 02 A hero built around reassurance, with a clear promise, a visible rating and two direct ways to book: request online or call.
  3. 03 Core values and treatment sections that explain care in plain language, from general and cosmetic to pediatric dentistry.
  4. 04 Patient story cards that pair each treatment journey with clear, scannable result figures.
  5. 05 A responsive Webflow build with a visit section for address, opening hours and booking, so patients have everything in one place.
Outcome

Calm from
the first click

A practice website designed to answer patients’ questions before they ask, and to make booking the natural next step.

  • A welcoming first impression for nervous and first-time patients
  • Clear treatment information that builds trust before the first visit
  • A booking path in every section, by call, email or online request
